## External compatibility reports known at release time

Earlier HLS startup and playback reports motivated bounded acquisition and
recovery work. They do not establish that every upstream timeout has one cause
or that an unavailable source can be recovered. The native playback owner now
fences producer failures to the session that created them; retry remains
bounded and scoped to the selected channel.

## Compatibility tiers, and what moved

The established single-view playback path remains the default. System
television discovery and handoff are available for supported sources. HLS
startup, producer retirement and same-channel recovery preserve the active
selection and do not introduce automatic channel switching.

Classic retains its guarded Search and Favorite controls. The settings schema
appends `nativeMultiviewService` after the existing settings; its descriptor is
sensitive and configured values are redacted from reads and save receipts.

The private-v1 multiview integration is experimental and opt-in. It depends on
an explicitly configured service, has not been accepted on a physical TV, and
is not a stable-release qualification claim. Portable profile data does not
authorize or carry its installation-bound service credential.

Runtime/App ownership and the optional multiview payload are heap-backed,
avoiding deep stack use without adding per-frame allocation. Host fixtures
mirror that production ownership. The regression suite validates the 46-setting
schema order and the new service redaction contract; these checks do not prove
physical playback.
