# OTTPlay LG 0.4.34 candidate audit

## Device test evidence

Version 0.4.33 rejected a live H.264 stream with two six-channel AC-3
tracks during inspection, before opening the decoder. A retry on the same
receiver reproduced the refusal. Both the media playlist and transport
segment were reachable.

## External compatibility reports known at release time

The stream inspection reported 1920×1080 H.264 at 24 fps and two 48 kHz
AC-3 tracks. An independent generated fixture exercises selection by
transport PID and verifies the audio bytes for each track.

## Compatibility tiers, and what moved

The native live HLS path admits H.264 with AC-3 in MPEG-TS when the
receiver supports the selected stream. The app reuses its compressed
audio and H.264 video paths.

## Known issues at release, with provenance

Receiver support depends on the native decoder and the selected stream.
Four simultaneous HD channels also depend on source and receiver capacity.

## Release configuration

The ARM package updates the primary application com.openottplay.player.native.
