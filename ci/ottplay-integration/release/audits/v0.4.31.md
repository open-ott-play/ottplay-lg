## Boundaries

Classic and Modern remain presentations of one source-owned application state.
Derived playable/container/action roles do not grant another action, locator or
credential. Management controls retain their ordinary setting owners. A
presentation digest retires stale card/reader memory; it is not a preview token.

All/category shelves reference canonical rows and retain the existing aggregate
50,000-occurrence and focus-key budgets. The complete flat grid remains the
fallback for invalid identities, reserved-ID collision or exceeded bounds.
Temporary Local sessions retain their existing source fence and flat cards.
Representative category artwork is bounded display data, not folder metadata.

HTTP live classification requires an existing live witness. Neither a title nor
a provider name makes an unknown finite item live. VPortal artwork selection
reuses admitted source URLs without auth-header borrowing or new requests.
Local art retention is bounded, positive-only and checked against current profile,
canonical locator and authorized root. Its cache grants no file access, does not
retain descriptions or media copies, and does not widen Favorite authority.

Classic Back uses the typed picker registry before a covering player's Back can
consume it. Contextual Left changes only its existing Categories action; paging,
volume, kiosk and settings-save policy remain owned by their existing paths.
