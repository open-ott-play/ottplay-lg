## Compatibility tiers, and what moved

Producer retirement now fences the complete terminal publication transaction,
including transport details, under the native owner lock. Old-owner completions
cannot poison replacement state. Trusted exhausted segment availability and
known body-transport failures retain typed outcomes for existing same-channel
recovery. Cancellation, malformed media, refused access and retry budgets keep
their original policy.

Classic reuses existing Search/Favorite actions in its compact footer and retains
Browse overflow access. New installations use Classic; explicit stored mode
choices remain intact. Remote status gains a bounded, source-free in-memory
command-family history. Status reads do not consume the retained evidence.

## Known issues at release, with provenance

A local first-open redirect fixture intermittently reached its unchanged two-second
budget before playlist inspection. The unchanged fetch group and a controlled
fresh-process attempt passed. The local serial media run is therefore not
reported as an entirely green gate; fresh Linux CI is required. Production and
fixture timeout limits were not increased.

Complete physical equivalence with the PC player is not certified. Native PiP,
unsupported browser playback settings and legacy explicit-lock migration remain
separate platform/migration work. EPG completeness depends on actual guide data.
Sustained picture/sound, remote pointer response and recovery require later
acceptance on the exact package. The optional command history describes ingress
and local completion only, not the initiating human or internal/local actions.
