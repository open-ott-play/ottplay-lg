## Compatibility tiers, and what moved

Checked transient playlist attempts retain their immutable resource and original
absolute deadline. Native playback keeps committed A/V state during these
attempts. Source-owned preparation and disconnect recovery use existing
continuity and admission, never alternate-channel selection or RPC replay.
Retry spacing is capped; finite-file failure handling remains separate.
Healthy-budget renewal uses sustained current-owner real-position evidence.
Plain live HLS retains its logical entry across inspection and a bounded fresh
startup resolution, rather than reusing a temporary inspected media address.
No cookie-based cause is asserted: metadata-only probes observed both successful
and timed-out fresh chains with and without cookies, and an older child address
returned a client-session refusal. These probes do not establish sustained
playback or identical receiver source credentials. Permanent server refusals
remain distinct from the existing transient HTTP allowlist.
The loading-orbit geometry shares one widget constructor.

## Known issues at release, with provenance

Physical television rendering, Magic Remote behavior, audible synchronization
and sustained stream recovery remain device acceptance work. A bounded fetch
or source-owned retry is not evidence that an unreachable source can be made
available. Auth, trust, malformed or unknown failures are intentionally not
reclassified as recoverable networking. Existing native codec and local
multiview restrictions remain documented in their respective guides.
