# OTTPlay LG 0.4.20 candidate

Fix external HLS playback remaining paused after an internal rebuffer despite downloaded media being available. Retain validated producer-owned interval evidence while bounded access-unit queues are full, including when the producer parks before the native clock is paused. Resume still requires actual queued/fed media and decoder readiness.

Preserve viewer Pause/Resume, source retirement and seek ownership. Publish seek targets atomically with revocation of old interval evidence. Continue accepting multiplexed segments without default audio packets under the existing media policy, without treating them as complete recovery evidence.

Retain the System television / LG Channels source and channel-only controller bindings. A new system-TV handoff must also wait for abandoned native loads to finish, in addition to releasing the active decoder. Controller pairing and channel availability depend on the connected television and region.

The correction applies to the native LG access-unit pipeline. Browser and Tauri playback retain their existing engine recovery. Host tests and ARM packaging are distinct from physical television acceptance; no stable qualification is claimed for this candidate.

Package SHA-256: `__IPK_SHA256__`.

After downloading the release assets into one directory, verify them with `shasum -a 256 -c ottplay-lg-0.4.20.sha256` before installation.
