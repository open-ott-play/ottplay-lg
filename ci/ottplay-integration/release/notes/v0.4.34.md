# OTTPlay LG 0.4.34

Live MPEG-TS HLS channels with H.264 video and Dolby Digital (AC-3)
audio can now use the native player on a compatible LG TV. The player
preserves the selected audio track and its surround sound. It checks the
receiver's codec capabilities before opening the stream.

On-device multiview now gives each window its own live HLS session. The
player can show each window’s channel and current programme in its timed
footer. The focused window plays at full player volume. Set the background
volume under Settings → Interface, or with `ott NAME setting multiviewBackgroundVolume 20`.
Detailed status reports count new video frames drawn per window.

Grid, picture-in-picture and perimeter layouts now meet edge to edge. Each
window fills its tile using the source's display aspect ratio. A session-limited
HLS gateway can advertise support for switching one channel while its neighbors
keep playing. The TV can also recover a previously playing channel after its
gateway confirms the live session has expired.

In Classic, Channel list is the main screen. Back from Channel list or
Favorites asks whether to exit the player. Choosing No keeps the selected row
and current playback. Providers and profiles are available from Menu, and an
empty Channel list offers a direct route to provider setup.

Classic remembers where you opened Categories, including after a visit to
Settings. Back returns to the same fullscreen video or channel list. Local
torrent and Stremio settings also return to the menu that opened them. The exit
question places its Yes and No buttons directly below the question. Magic
Remote clicks outside those buttons are now recorded and replayed correctly.
