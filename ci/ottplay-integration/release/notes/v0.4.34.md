# OTTPlay LG 0.4.34

Live MPEG-TS HLS channels with H.264 video and Dolby Digital (AC-3)
audio can now use the native player on a compatible LG TV. The player
preserves the selected audio track and its surround sound. It checks the
receiver's codec capabilities before opening the stream.

On-device multiview now gives each window its own live HLS session. The
player can show each window’s channel and current programme in its timed
footer. The focused window plays at full player volume. Set the background
volume under Settings → Interface, or with `ott NAME setting multiviewBackgroundVolume 20`.
Detailed status reports count new video frames drawn per window.
